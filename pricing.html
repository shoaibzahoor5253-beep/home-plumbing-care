Frequently Asked Questions

Why is this application completely free?

Home Plumbing Care is designed as an open-source, privacy-first web utility to help homeowners organize routine checks and avoid catastrophic water damage before it happens.

Will there ever be paid plans or subscription upgrades?

No. There are no paid plans, no premium tiers, no subscription fees, and no feature gates. Everything you see is free and will remain free.

Do I need to submit credit card information or sign up?

No account creation or payment information is ever requested. The application runs directly inside your browser and saves all your data locally using your browser's LocalStorage.

Can I export my data if I need to switch devices?

Yes. The app includes a built-in JSON backup and restore feature under the "Data" button, letting you download and restore your records anytime for free.
